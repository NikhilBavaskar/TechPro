How to Verify If Your Office CCTV Is Actually Recording (30-Day Storage Audit)

Over 35% of commercial establishments, diagnostic clinics, and retail stores in Akola only discover their DVR hard drive failed when police or insurance auditors request footage after an incident.

Critical Warning: Most Hikvision, CP Plus, and Dahua recorders keep showing live feeds on your screen even if the internal storage drive has seized, formatted with errors, or disconnected entirely.

Step 1: Check the HDD Management Menu

Right-click anywhere on your recorder's display monitor and log in using your admin pattern or password. Navigate to Main Menu > Storage > HDD Management.

  • Normal / Connected: The drive is healthy and recording.
  • Error / Unformatted / Offline: The drive has dead sectors or a broken SATA controller. Immediate servicing is required.

Step 2: The 28-Day Playback Test

Open the Playback screen. Select Camera 01 and jump backward on the calendar exactly 28 days. If the timeline is solid green/blue, recording has been continuous. If you see intermittent blank zones, your drive is running out of sector capacity or overheating.
