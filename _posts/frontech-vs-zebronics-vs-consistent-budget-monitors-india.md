# Frontech vs Zebronics vs Consistent: The Ultimate Sub-₹5,000 Monitor Shootout for Office & CCTV Setups

Walk into any local computer market in India—from Lamington Road in Mumbai and Nehru Place in Delhi to your local IT hardware dealer in Maharashtra—and ask for an affordable 21.5-inch or 22-inch LED monitor.

The dealer will instantly place three boxes on the counter: **Zebronics**, **Consistent**, and **Frontech**.

At first glance, their spec sheets look almost identical. All three claim Full HD (1920x1080) resolution, standard 16:9 widescreen formats, dual HDMI and VGA inputs, and wall-mounting slots. Most importantly, all three hover in the ₹3,200 to ₹4,800 price bracket—half the price of basic entry-level screens from Samsung, LG, or Dell.

However, when you mount these displays on an active retail billing counter or connect them to a 16-channel CCTV DVR running 24/7 in an unventilated room, their real differences surface immediately.

Below is an honest, technician-tested comparison of how these three Indian budget heavyweights perform in daily commercial and surveillance environments.

## 1. Zebronics: The Safe Consumer Choice with High Contrast

Zebronics is the most widely distributed and reviewed brand of the three. Models like the **Zeb-EA122** are popular on online marketplaces due to consistent quality control.

### The Strengths

- **Sharp Text Rendering for Accounting:** The contrast ratio on Zebronics panels is tuned sharp out of the box. If your primary work involves reading black text on white backgrounds—such as Tally Prime, busy Excel ledgers, or billing software—text edges are crisp and easy to read.
- **Slim Design & Predictable Build:** The chassis on newer Zebronics units is remarkably thin, making them aesthetically pleasing on front-office counters.
- **Easy Availability:** Replacement power adapters and HDMI cables are easy to source, and the brand is recognized everywhere.

### The Drawbacks

- **Limited Viewing Angles:** Most budget Zebronics 21.5" units use standard TN-grade panels with \~140° viewing angles. If you look at the screen from below or from a sharp side angle, colors shift quickly.
- **Only 1-Year Warranty:** Unlike its competitors, Zebronics typically offers only a 1-year limited warranty on entry-level displays.

## 2. Consistent: The Local IT Industry Favorite (Best Warranty)

Consistent has built strong credibility among Indian local hardware engineers, system integrators, and CCTV installers. Models like the **Consistent CTM2200** are engineered specifically for commercial and B2B deployments.

### The Strengths

- **3-Year Manufacturer Warranty:** This is Consistent’s biggest competitive edge. While Zebronics covers you for 12 months, Consistent backs most of their LED monitors with a full **3-year warranty**, providing much better long-term protection for offices and institutions.
- **Superior Viewing Angles (VA/IPS Panels):** Many Consistent 21.5" and 22" models feature VA or borderless IPS panels offering up to 178° viewing angles. When mounted high on a wall for CCTV monitoring, security personnel can view camera feeds clearly from across the room without color washing out.
- **Integrated Audio:** Several Consistent models include basic internal 2W speakers. While not meant for music, they are practical for hearing Windows notification chimes or CCTV motion buzzer alerts without needing external desktop speakers.

### The Drawbacks

- **Bulkier Wall-Mount Profile:** Some Consistent models have rear port placements that face straight out rather than downward, requiring a bit more wall clearance when using slim VESA brackets.

## 3. Frontech: The Ultra-Budget Workhorse (Wholesale Value)

Frontech is a classic name in Indian computer peripherals. If keeping upfront hardware procurement costs as low as humanly possible is your mandate, Frontech is usually the lowest-priced option on a dealer's quotation sheet.

### The Strengths

- **Lowest Upfront Acquisition Cost:** In offline wholesale distributor markets, Frontech 21.5" and 22" monitors often undercut rivals by ₹300 to ₹600 per unit, which adds up when outfitting a 20-computer educational lab.
- **Decent Color Saturation:** Frontech panels produce punchy, vibrant primary colors, making them well-suited for general web browsing, school computer training, and retail shop POS setups.
- **3-Year Warranty on Select Lines:** Newer Frontech commercial and Ultima models now carry a 3-year warranty to match Consistent.

### The Drawbacks

- **Resolution Pitfalls (Buyer Beware!):** You must check model numbers carefully. While Zebronics and Consistent 21.5"/22" models are almost universally Full HD 1080p (1920x1080), **some budget Frontech 22" screens use older 16:10 aspect ratio panels with 1680 x 1050 (HD+) resolution**. If you connect a 1080p DVR to a 1680x1050 panel, camera feeds can look slightly stretched or blurry. Always verify that the box explicitly says **1920x1080 Full HD**.
- **Flimsier Table Stands:** The plastic desktop stands on entry-level Frontech units tend to have more wobble compared to Zebronics.

## Real-World Recommendation: Which Monitor Should You Buy?

### Scenario A: For Continuous CCTV Surveillance (NVR / DVR Wall Mount)

- **The Winner:** **Consistent 21.5” / 22”**
- **Why:** When a monitor is wall-mounted high in an office or security cabin, wide viewing angles are mandatory so you can monitor cameras while sitting down. Consistent’s 178° viewing angles, 3-year warranty, and built-in buzzer speakers make it the most durable choice for 24/7 continuous operation.

### Scenario B: For Busy Retail Billing Counters & Accounting (Tally / POS)

- **The Winner:** **Zebronics 21.5” (Zeb-EA122)**
- **Why:** If the operator sits directly in front of the screen for 8 hours a day, Zebronics delivers the sharpest black-and-white font contrast with minimal glare. The ultra-slim bezel leaves maximum counter space for receipt printers, cash drawers, and barcode scanners.

### Scenario C: For School Labs & Bulk Commercial Procurements

- **The Winner:** **Frontech 22” (Verified 1080p variants) or Consistent**
- **Why:** When outfitting a 15 to 30-system laboratory or branch office on a fixed budget, the lower per-unit cost combined with a 3-year warranty offers maximum return on investment.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I connect an older CPU with only a blue VGA port to these monitors?

Yes. All three brands (Zebronics, Consistent, and Frontech) feature standard legacy 15-pin VGA ports alongside modern HDMI ports. You can plug older desktop cabinets directly into the monitor using a standard VGA cable without purchasing extra converter adapters.

### Do these budget monitors support standard VESA wall mounting?

Yes. Almost all 21.5" and 22" models from these three manufacturers feature standard 75 x 75 mm VESA screw holes on the back panel, allowing you to bolt them to fixed wall brackets or adjustable swivel arms.

### Is a 21.5-inch or 24-inch monitor better for office work?

For small office desks, cash counters, and CCTV walls, **21.5 inches is the ideal size**. It offers sharp pixel density in 1080p Full HD without taking up excessive table space. 24-inch monitors are better suited for creative editing, multi-window programming, or larger executive desks.
