## Component Deep Dive: Why These Exact Parts?

### 1. Processor: AMD Ryzen 5 5600 (₹10,400)

The 6-core, 12-thread Ryzen 5 5600 remains a strong value processor in India. Because it features a full **32MB of L3 cache** (unlike the cache-cut 5500 or 5600G), 1% low framerates in competitive titles remain rock steady without micro-stutters.

- **Thermal Bonus:** Operating at a modest 65W TDP, the bundled **Wraith Stealth cooler** easily keeps temperatures below 72°C during standard gaming sessions, saving you ₹1,500 to ₹2,500 on an aftermarket air cooler.

### 2. Graphics Card: AMD Radeon RX 6600 8GB GDDR6 (₹19,500)

In the sub-₹20,000 GPU bracket, the RX 6600 has no equivalent competition:

- NVIDIA’s RTX 3050 in this price segment is significantly slower.
- The Intel Arc A750 is capable, but its higher power draw (225W) demands a more expensive 650W PSU and produces extra cabinet heat.
- The RX 6600 consumes only **132W under full load**, features 8GB of dedicated VRAM (mandatory for modern textures), and handles modern 1080p gaming smoothly.

### 3. Storage: WD Blue SN580 1TB NVMe SSD (₹5,600)

A modern gaming rig requires substantial storage; modern titles and operating systems quickly fill smaller drives. The **WD Blue SN580 1TB** provides up to 4,150 MB/s read speeds, reliable 3D TLC flash memory, and a 600 TBW endurance rating, eliminating loading screens and ensuring responsive boot times.

### 4. RAM: 16GB (8GB x 2) DDR4 3200MHz Dual Channel (₹3,100)

Never run a single 16GB RAM stick on an AMD Ryzen processor. Ryzen's Infinity Fabric architecture depends heavily on memory bandwidth. Running two 8GB sticks in **Dual Channel mode** yields an immediate 15% to 25% bump in minimum framerates compared to single-channel configurations.

### 5. Power Supply: 550W 80 Plus Bronze (₹3,500)

Never cheap out on the power supply unit. A cheap ₹800 non-certified cabinet SMPS lacks over-voltage protection (OVP) and can fail under typical Indian power fluctuations, risking damage to the motherboard and GPU. A Tier-C rated **550W 80 Plus Bronze PSU** gives this 300W system plenty of power headroom and clean, regulated current.

## 4 Costly Budget PC Mistakes to Avoid in India

1. **Buying an APU (like the 5600G) When You Already Have a GPU:** The Ryzen 5 5600G costs almost the same as the 5600, but has half the L3 cache (16MB vs 32MB) and is locked to PCIe 3.0. Always pick the standard **Ryzen 5 5600** when pairing with a dedicated graphics card.
2. **Sacrificing Airflow for RGB Lighting:** In non-air-conditioned Indian rooms where ambient temperatures reach 38°C–44°C, avoid cabinets with solid front glass panels. Always buy a cabinet with a **perforated front mesh** to maintain continuous airflow across the graphics card and processor.
3. **Forgetting to Enable XMP / DOCP in BIOS:** After assembling your PC, your DDR4 RAM will default to running at 2133MHz or 2400MHz. You must enter the BIOS screen during startup and enable **DOCP / XMP Profile 1** to run the memory at its advertised 3200MHz speed.
4. **Plugging the Display Cable into the Motherboard:** A classic beginner error: always connect your HDMI or DisplayPort cable directly to the **horizontal ports on the RX 6600 graphics card**, never the vertical video port on the motherboard.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can this build be upgraded to 32GB RAM in the future?

Yes. If you choose a motherboard with 2 RAM slots, you can later swap the two 8GB sticks for two 16GB sticks. Alternatively, spending an extra ₹800 on a 4-slot motherboard (such as the Gigabyte B450M DS3H WiFi or MSI B550M PRO-VDH) allows you to simply insert two additional 8GB sticks later on.

### Is an Intel Core i5 alternative available in the same budget?

Yes. You can swap the Ryzen 5 5600 and B450 motherboard for an **Intel Core i5-12400F** paired with an **H610M DDR4 motherboard**. Gaming and creative performance between the two platforms is within 3% of each other, so feel free to pick whichever CPU-motherboard combination is cheaper at your local retailer.

### Will this PC run on a home inverter?

A standard home square-wave inverter will trigger safety reboots when power cuts out. If you experience frequent power cuts, pair the machine with an **inexpensive 600VA–1100VA line-interactive desktop UPS** to give you 5–10 minutes to save your work and safely shut down.
